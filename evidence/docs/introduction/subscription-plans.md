# Subscription plans

> Compare the Free, Pro, Business, and Enterprise subscription plans, see which features and credit tiers each plan includes, and find the student and teacher discount.

Lovable has a Free plan and three paid plans: Pro, Business, and Enterprise. This page compares what each plan includes. For current prices and credit tiers, see [Lovable pricing](https://lovable.dev/pricing).

> **[Change or cancel your subscription](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/manage-subscription.md)**
>
> Upgrade, downgrade, switch between monthly and annual billing, or cancel, and see what happens to your credits afterward.

> **[Invoices and billing](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/invoices-and-billing.md)**
>
> Find and download invoices and receipts, update your payment method, and edit billing and tax details.

For how credits work, what uses credits, and how to add credits with top-ups, see [Credits and usage](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage.md).

> [!TIP]
>
> **Need help with a plan?** For plan and billing questions, [contact the support team](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/support-policy.md#contact-support). For Enterprise pricing and contracts, [contact sales](https://lovable.dev/enterprise).

## Compare plans

Each plan is designed for a different kind of user.

| Plan | Best for |
| :- | :- |
| Free | Trying Lovable and building smaller projects |
| Pro | Fast-moving teams building together in real time |
| Business | Growing departments that need advanced controls and governance |
| Enterprise | Large organizations that need flexibility, scale, and governance |

*\*After you reach the monthly cap, Lovable stops granting the daily 5 credits for the rest of that calendar month and resumes on the 1st at 00:00 UTC. For example, on Free, you receive daily build credits for the first 6 days each month (6 × 5 = 30), then none until the next month starts.*

> [!TIP]
>
> **Make the most of your included daily chat allowance and daily build credits**.
>
> Chat usage follows its own pricing model, including a per-message price and a daily chat allowance. Free, Pro, and Business plans include the **daily chat allowance** for messages in [Chats](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/chats.md) and [Chat mode](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/chat-mode.md), including Lovable in [Slack](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/lovable-for-slack.md) and [Telegram](https://docs.lovable.dev/tips-tricks/lovable-telegram-bot.md). It resets every day at **00:00 UTC** and does not roll over. See [Chat costs](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage.md#chat-costs).
>
> Free, Pro, and Business plans also include **5 daily build credits** at no extra cost. They reset every day at **00:00 UTC** and do not roll over.
>
> On Free plans, daily build credits are capped at **30 per calendar month**. After you reach the monthly cap, daily build credits resume on the 1st of the next calendar month at **00:00 UTC**.
>
> Pro and Business plans do not have a monthly cap for daily build credits.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Free, Pro, and Business** workspaces include monthly Cloud and AI grants so you can host apps, run their built-in backend (Cloud), and use AI features in deployed apps without spending general credits on that usage first.
>
> **Enterprise** terms vary by contract. Enterprise plans do not include the daily chat allowance, daily build credits, or the monthly Cloud and AI grants. Contact your account team for account-specific details.

## Subscription tiers and billing cycles

Pro and Business plans come in monthly credit tiers, from 100 to 10,000 credits per month. You choose the tier from the credits dropdown on the plan card in [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing).

Each tier is billed monthly or annually. Annual billing gives you two months free compared with paying monthly. Monthly plan credits on annual billing are still issued monthly and stay valid until 1 month after the annual billing period ends.

Current prices for every tier and billing cycle are on [Lovable pricing](https://lovable.dev/pricing). To move to a different tier or billing cycle, see [Change or cancel your subscription](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/manage-subscription.md).

## Student and teacher discount

Students and teachers at universities, colleges, and schools get 50% off for up to 12 months on the Pro plan with 100 credits per month, billed monthly. Lovable verifies your status on [lovable.dev/students](https://lovable.dev/students) by checking the domain of your account's email address (the part after the @) against a list of academic institutions. Verification is available only to workspaces on the Free plan, and each person can use the discount once.

**1. Log in with your academic email**

Create your Lovable account with your institution's email address, or log in to the account that already uses it. Lovable verifies the email address your account is registered with, and that address cannot be changed. If your existing account uses a personal email, create a new account with your academic email. See the tip below for moving your projects into the new account's workspace.

**2. Verify your status**

Open [lovable.dev/students](https://lovable.dev/students) and select **Verify student status**. Choose **Student** or **Teacher**, select **Next**, answer the short questions about your studies or teaching, confirm your status, and select **Verify Status Now**.

**3. Subscribe to Pro for Students**

After verification, the same dialog shows the **Pro for Students** plan at the discounted price. The plan name also covers verified teachers. Select **Upgrade** and complete checkout. To return to the plan later, select **Get your discount** on [lovable.dev/students](https://lovable.dev/students). Your verification stays valid for 12 months, so subscribe within that time. The discount then applies for up to 12 months from the start of your subscription, or until you cancel. Lovable does not offer the discount again after you cancel.

The discount has these limits:

* It applies only to the Pro plan with 100 credits per month, billed monthly. Higher Pro credit tiers, annual billing, and other plans are not available with the discount.
* Credit top-ups are charged at standard prices.
* The discount is tied to the account that verified and cannot be transferred to another account or person.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **My institution's email domain is not recognized**
>
> If the verification dialog shows **Invalid email**, either your address is a personal one, which Lovable cannot verify, or your institution's domain is not on the list yet. For an institution domain, select **Why did this happen?** and then **Request review**. Lovable reviews requested domains and adds eligible institutions to the list. If Lovable adds yours, verify again from [lovable.dev/students](https://lovable.dev/students).

> [!TIP]
>
> If your projects are in a workspace owned by an account with a personal email, verify with a new account that uses your academic email and subscribe there. Then invite your personal account to the new workspace, because you can move a project only between workspaces you belong to. Logged in as your personal account, select **Move workspace** in each project's settings to move the project there. See [Move a project to another workspace](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/projects/settings.md#move-a-project-to-another-workspace).

## FAQ

> **How do I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my plan?**
>
> You change your plan yourself in [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing). To cancel, select **Manage** on your plan card, then **Downgrade to free**. For the steps for each change and what happens to your credits afterward, see [Change or cancel your subscription](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/manage-subscription.md).

> **Where do I find my invoices?**
>
> Go to [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing), select **Manage** on your plan card, then **Invoices & payments**. Lovable opens the Stripe billing portal, where you download invoices and receipts. See [Invoices and billing](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/invoices-and-billing.md).

> **Can I pay annually?**
>
> Yes. Pro and Business plans are billed monthly or annually. Annual billing gives you two months free compared with paying monthly, and your monthly plan credits stay valid until 1 month after the annual billing period ends. See [Switch between monthly and annual billing](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/manage-subscription.md#switch-between-monthly-and-annual-billing).

> **Do Enterprise plans include daily build credits and the monthly Cloud and AI grants?**
>
> No. Enterprise plans do not include the daily chat allowance, daily build credits, or the monthly Cloud and AI grants. Enterprise credit terms are set by contract. Contact your account team for account-specific details.

> **Is there a discount for students and teachers?**
>
> Yes. Verified students and teachers get 50% off the Pro plan with 100 credits per month, billed monthly, for up to 12 months. Verify your academic email on [lovable.dev/students](https://lovable.dev/students) while your workspace is on the Free plan, then subscribe to **Pro for Students**. See [Student and teacher discount](#student-and-teacher-discount).
