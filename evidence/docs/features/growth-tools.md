# Growth tools

> Get help finding customers, improving search visibility, creating marketing content, and running ads on Google, YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram.

Growth tools help you find customers and grow your business with Lovable. You can explore how to reach your first users, improve how people find you, create marketing content, or manage paid campaigns. Lovable recommends an approach based on your app, audience, and circumstances, and guides you through the work.

Start with a question like “How do I get more customers?”. You do not need a marketing plan or a goal worked out in advance. Lovable helps you decide where to start.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Growth tools are available on all plans.
>
> Google Ads, Google Business Profile, and ads on Facebook and Instagram use [app + chat connectors](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/app-connectors.md). Your workspace's connector settings determine who can connect these accounts. On Enterprise plans, an admin must allow access first. See [Who can create connections and clients](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/admin-controls.md#who-can-create-connections-and-clients).

## Start with a growth plan

Ask Lovable in your own words. A simple question is enough:

```text wrap theme={null}
How can I get more people to use my app?
```

You can also open **More → Analytics** and select **Get more visitors**. Lovable receives your current traffic alongside the request.

Lovable considers what it knows about your app and researches what would help your business. That can include competitors, what people search for, where your audience spends time, and campaigns you already run. The research and recommendations depend on your market, location, and stage of growth.

The plan can include SEO and AI search, outreach, content, a Business Profile, or paid ads. Lovable may recommend a [custom domain](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/custom-domain.md) to give your business a recognizable address and build its search presence on a domain you own. You do not need a custom domain to begin planning.

Lovable asks questions when it needs more context and helps you choose a direction. You can refine the plan, try another approach, or return later to continue. Research and prepared content are saved in your project's **Files** view.

## How approvals work

Lovable asks for approval before publishing through connected accounts or creating and changing campaigns. You decide the ad budget and approve when spending can start. See [Approve and launch](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/ads.md#approve-and-launch) for the differences between Google Ads and ads on Facebook and Instagram.

Posts and messages for social media or outreach are drafts for you to publish or send. With a suitable connected account, such as Google Business Profile, Lovable can publish supported posts and replies after you approve them.

For changes to your app, such as new pages or tracking, you can review the preview before publishing.

## What it costs

Lovable adds no extra fees for using Growth tools. The normal costs of chatting with Lovable apply. See [Usage costs](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage.md#usage-costs) for details.

Paid ads cost money separately. Google or Meta charges the payment method on your advertising account for the ads you run. Lovable does not collect that ad spend or charge additional fees on it. You choose the budget and set up billing with the advertising provider.

## See your results

You can follow progress in Lovable and ask for help interpreting the results:

* **[Analytics](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/analytics.md)** shows visits by channel and, with Google Search Console connected, clicks, impressions, and searches. The **Ads** tab shows campaign spend, impressions, clicks, and conversions over time alongside your app's paid traffic.
* **[Campaign results in Files](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/ads.md#campaign-results-in-files)**: the collection that represents a campaign shows its status and results, which files it uses, and their review status.
* **Chat with Lovable** to evaluate what is working, generate a report, or decide what to change. See [What to expect after launch](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/ads.md#what-to-expect-after-launch).

Lovable proposes campaign changes for your approval. Google and Meta also optimize ad delivery within the campaigns you run. Results depend on your offer, market, and budget.

## Limitations

Growth tools help you prepare and carry out marketing work, with some steps completed outside Lovable:

* **Publishing and sending depend on the connection.** Without a suitable connected account, you send outreach and publish social posts yourself. Growth tools do not run email campaigns or referral programs.
* **Ad formats have different setup requirements.** Lovable helps you select and set up Google Ads campaign types like Search, Performance Max, and Demand Gen based on your goals. Lovable also guides you through setting up campaigns on Facebook and Instagram. Ask Lovable about other formats and what can be prepared or managed through your connection. See [Choose where your ads appear](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/ads.md#choose-where-your-ads-appear).
* **Live results need a live site.** You can plan before publishing, but website traffic, search visibility, and campaigns that send visitors to your app need a published site.

## FAQ

Find answers to common questions about getting started and reviewing progress.

> **Do I need to know what marketing to do?**
>
> No. Tell Lovable what you are working on, or ask how to get more customers.
> Lovable helps you understand your audience, choose a direction, and work
> through the next steps.

> **Does my app need to be published?**
>
> Not to start planning or preparing content. Publish your app before
> measuring website traffic or sending visitors to it through ads.

> **What does it cost?**
>
> Lovable adds no extra fees for Growth tools. Normal Lovable usage costs
> apply. If you run paid ads, Google or Meta charges you separately for your
> ad spend. See [What it costs](#what-it-costs).

> **When does Lovable recommend paid ads?**
>
> When they fit your business, audience, and budget. Prior sales or sign-ups
> are not required. If you are still exploring demand, Lovable can help plan a
> smaller test. It may also suggest improving your offer or website before
> paying to send visitors there.

> **Can Lovable help after a campaign launches?**
>
> Yes. Lovable can evaluate performance, generate reports, and suggest
> improvements. You approve campaign changes. It does not continually change
> campaigns on its own.

> **How long until I see results?**
>
> Timing depends on the approach and your market. Search visibility can
> continue improving over weeks or months. New paid ad campaigns on Google and
> Meta go through a learning period. During this period, the platform gathers
> data, learns about your customers, and finds the most efficient way to
> invest your budget before it ramps up fully. Lovable can help you analyze
> results, understand what to expect, and decide whether to make a change.
