# Change or cancel your subscription

> Upgrade or downgrade your plan or credit tier, switch between monthly and annual billing, or cancel your Lovable subscription, and see what happens to your credits and features afterward.

Workspace owners and admins change or cancel the subscription in [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing).

Select **Manage** on your plan card to open the **Manage plan** dialog.

![Plans & credit usage with an arrow pointing to the Manage button on the plan card](https://mintcdn.com/lovable-f9060f1e/T3OMLZ3D9k10122P/images/plans-credit-usage-manage.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=T3OMLZ3D9k10122P\&q=85\&s=ad1eaf79a537bf96e974e4feb581e1c0)

The dialog shows your current plan and daily credits, with **Upgrade plan**, **Downgrade to free**, **Edit billing information**, and **Invoices & payments**, and a **Gift card balance** card where you can redeem a gift card.

To check which plan your workspace is on, or whether a new subscription has activated, you can also [ask Lovable in the project chat](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage.md#tracking-credit-usage). Lovable can tell you your plan but cannot change it.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Looking for something else?** This page is about one workspace's plan and subscription.
>
> * **Leave Lovable entirely.** A Free workspace has no subscription to cancel. To remove your account and its data, see [Delete account](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/delete-account.md).
> * **Remove one workspace.** To delete a workspace and keep your account, see [Delete a workspace](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/delete-workspace.md).
> * **Find an invoice or change a payment method.** See [Invoices and billing](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/invoices-and-billing.md).
> * **Compare plans and credit tiers.** See [Subscription plans](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/subscription-plans.md).

## Upgrade your plan or credit tier

Use these steps to move to a higher plan, or to a higher credit tier on Pro or Business. If you consistently run out of subscription credits before your renewal date, upgrading is usually more cost-effective than repeated top-ups. For one-time top-ups and auto top-up, see [Credit top-ups](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credit-top-ups.md). For how credits are spent, see [Usage costs](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage.md#usage-costs).

**1. Open Plans & credit usage**

Go to [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing).

**2. Choose the plan and credit tier**

On the **Pro** or **Business** plan card, choose your desired credit amount from the credits dropdown.

**3. Upgrade**

Select **Upgrade current plan** for a higher credit tier on your current plan, or **Upgrade** for a higher plan. Then complete the upgrade in Stripe.

The upgrade takes effect when you complete it.

> [!NOTE]
>
> When you upgrade from Free, you receive the full monthly allocation of the plan you choose. When you upgrade from one paid plan or credit tier to a higher one, you do not receive a full new grant. Your monthly allocation is updated, and you receive the difference. For example, upgrading from 100 credits to 200 credits adds 100 credits, not 200. Your existing unused credits keep their original expiry date.

## Downgrade your plan or credit tier

Use these steps to move from Business to Pro, or to a lower credit tier on Pro or Business. To move to the Free plan instead, see [Cancel your subscription](#cancel-your-subscription).

**1. Open Plans & credit usage**

Go to [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing).

**2. Choose the plan and credit tier**

On the **Pro** or **Business** plan card, choose your desired credit amount from the credits dropdown.

**3. Downgrade**

Select **Downgrade**, then complete the downgrade in Lovable and in the Stripe portal.

Your current plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. Until then, your plan card shows the pending change with a **Cancel changes** button.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Downgrading to a lower plan or credit tier keeps your existing credits**
>
> When you downgrade to a lower plan or credit tier, including from Business to Pro, your unused monthly plan credits and rollover credits stay available until their original expiry date. Your new monthly credit allocation applies from your next billing period.

## Switch between monthly and annual billing

You can switch a Pro or Business plan between monthly and annual billing. Annual billing gives you two months free compared with paying monthly, and your monthly plan credits stay valid until 1 month after the annual billing period ends.

**1. Open Plans & credit usage**

Go to [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing).

**2. Select the billing period**

Above the plan cards, select **Yearly**. The plan cards update to show the discounted monthly rate for annual billing.

**3. Switch to annual**

On your current plan card, select **Switch to annual** and confirm the change in checkout.

The button appears only for the credit tier you are on. If you select a different credit tier from the dropdown at the same time, the button reads **Upgrade** or **Downgrade** instead, and the tier change and the switch to annual billing happen together.

When you switch to annual billing, you are charged for the annual plan at checkout. Monthly plan credits continue to be granted each month.

To return to monthly billing, select **Monthly** above the plan cards, then select **Switch to monthly** on your current plan card and confirm the change in checkout.

## Cancel your subscription

Canceling your paid subscription moves your workspace to the Free plan at the end of your current billing period. **Plans & credit usage** has no cancel button, and you do not need to contact support. You cancel by downgrading to Free yourself.

A workspace that is already on Free has no subscription to cancel. To leave Lovable entirely, [delete your account](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/delete-account.md) instead.

**1. Open Plans & credit usage**

Go to [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing).

**2. Open the Manage plan dialog**

Select **Manage** on your plan card.

**3. Downgrade to free**

Select **Downgrade to free** and complete the downgrade flow.

![The Manage plan dialog with the Downgrade to free button highlighted](https://mintcdn.com/lovable-f9060f1e/T3OMLZ3D9k10122P/images/manage-plan-dialog.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=T3OMLZ3D9k10122P\&q=85\&s=97d02f333d9c966bdf52630d75b86ad1)

Your current plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, your workspace moves to Free.

Your published apps stay live on Free. Lovable may unpublish one later for inactivity, when the app has had no visits and no workspace-owner login for 12 months or more and has no custom domain. Lovable emails the workspace owners and admins and keeps the project, so you can wake its backend and publish again. See [Does my published site expire?](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/publish.md#does-my-published-site-expire).

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Downgrading to Free freezes subscription credits**
>
> If you downgrade to the Free plan, unexpired credits granted through your paid subscription cannot be used while your workspace is on Free. This includes monthly plan credits and rollover credits.
>
> If you upgrade to a paid plan again before those credits expire, you can continue using them until their original expiry date.
>
> Top-up credits you purchased while subscribed and any bonus credits can still be used on Free until they expire.

Until the period ends, the **Manage plan** dialog shows **Cancel downgrade** in place of **Downgrade to free**. Select it to keep your current plan.

> [!NOTE]
>
> The credits dropdown on the plan card only lists credit tiers, so it cannot be used to move to Free. Use **Manage → Downgrade to free** instead.

### Cancel from your phone

The [Lovable mobile app](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/lovable-mobile-app.md) has no cancel option, so on a phone you cancel in the browser. Open **lovable.dev** in your phone's browser, go to [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing), and follow the same steps. This applies on both iOS and Android.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Subscribed through the iOS app?**
>
> Plans purchased inside the iOS app go through the App Store and work differently: each purchase covers one month and does not renew automatically, so you have nothing to cancel. Your plan card in **Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage** shows **Managed by App Store** in place of the **Manage** button, along with the date your plan runs until. When that date passes, your workspace moves to the Free plan on its own, and you are not charged again unless you make a new purchase.
>
> Because these plans do not renew, they also do not appear under **Settings → \[Your name] → Subscriptions** on your iPhone or iPad. See [Lovable mobile app](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/lovable-mobile-app.md#buying-credits-and-upgrading-plans-on-mobile) for more on iOS and Android billing differences.

## What happens when you change or cancel

This table shows when each change takes effect and what happens to your monthly plan credits and rollover credits. Top-up credits and bonus credits are not affected by any plan change.

| Change | When it takes effect | What happens to your credits |
| :- | :- | :- |
| Upgrade to a higher plan or credit tier | When you complete the upgrade | <ul><li>From Free, you receive the full monthly allocation of the plan you choose.</li><li>From a paid plan, you receive the difference between your previous and new monthly allocation. For example, upgrading from 100 to 200 credits adds 100 credits, not 200.</li><li>Your existing unused credits keep their original expiry date.</li></ul> |
| Downgrade to a lower plan or credit tier | End of the current billing period | <ul><li>Your unused monthly plan credits and rollover credits stay available until their original expiry date.</li><li>Your new monthly allocation applies from the next billing period.</li></ul> |
| Switch to annual billing | At checkout, when you are charged for the annual plan | <ul><li>Monthly plan credits are still issued monthly.</li><li>They stay valid until 1 month after the annual billing period ends.</li></ul> |
| Cancel (downgrade to Free) | End of the current billing period | <ul><li>Your unused monthly plan credits and rollover credits are frozen. You cannot use them on Free, and Lovable does not refund them.</li><li>If you upgrade to a paid plan again before they expire, you can use them until their original expiry date.</li><li>Top-up credits you purchased while subscribed and any bonus credits stay usable on Free until they expire.</li></ul> |

For credit expiry and rollover rules, see [Credits and usage](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage.md#general-credits).

### Features that stop working when you downgrade

When your workspace moves to a lower plan, features that are not included in the new plan stop working. Examples include:

* [**Scheduled security scans**](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/security-center#schedule-security-scans-enterprise-only) and [**scheduled data exports**](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/data-exports) (Enterprise) stop running. Stopped scans no longer use credits.
* **Verified email sign-up** (Business and Enterprise) stops automatically adding new sign-ups to your workspace. Existing members keep their access.
* **Announcement banners** (Business and Enterprise) stop showing to workspace members.
* **Publishing to your workspace** (Business and Enterprise): an app published to your whole workspace stays open to workspace members until you next publish changes from the publish dialog. Lovable then changes its website access to **Invite only** and warns you before you publish. In a custom audience, workspace and group entries no longer grant access after the downgrade, and invited people keep their access. See [What happens to my published app if I downgrade or cancel my plan?](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/publish.md#what-happens-to-my-published-app-if-i-downgrade-or-cancel-my-plan).
* **Publishing domain** (Enterprise): existing app addresses keep working, including when you publish updates. Apps without an address under the publishing domain cannot get one until you upgrade to Enterprise again. See [Publishing domain](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/publishing-domain.md).
* **Hiding the Lovable badge** (paid plans): if you hid the **Edit with Lovable** badge on a published project, your live site keeps it hidden until you publish again. On that next publish, Lovable warns you, then brings the badge back and disables the project's **Hide Lovable badge** setting. To hide the badge again after upgrading, enable the setting again in **Project settings → Publishing**.

For most features, Lovable keeps your configuration, so they resume automatically if you upgrade again. Plan-dependent workspace defaults, such as default project visibility and publishing permissions, reset to the new plan's defaults and are not restored by a later upgrade.

## FAQ

> **How do I cancel my Lovable subscription?**
>
> Go to [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing), select **Manage** on your plan card, then select **Downgrade to free** and complete the downgrade flow. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, then your workspace moves to Free. See [Cancel your subscription](#cancel-your-subscription).
>
> If your workspace is already on Free, you have nothing to cancel. To leave Lovable entirely, see [Delete account](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/delete-account.md).

> **When do plan changes take effect?**
>
> Upgrades take effect when you complete the upgrade. If you upgrade to a higher credit allocation, you receive the difference between your previous allocation and your new allocation.
>
> Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period. Your current plan remains active until then. See [What happens when you change or cancel](#what-happens-when-you-change-or-cancel).

> **What happens to my credits if I cancel my subscription?**
>
> Your current plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, your workspace moves to the Free plan and your unused monthly plan credits and rollover credits are frozen. You cannot use them on Free, and Lovable does not refund them. If you upgrade again before they expire, you can use them until their original expiry date.
>
> Top-up credits you purchased while subscribed and any bonus credits stay usable on Free until they expire.
>
> For more about credit expiry and rollover behavior, see [Credits and usage](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage.md#general-credits).

> **What happens to my unused credits if I change my plan or credit tier?**
>
> Your unused monthly plan credits and rollover credits stay available until their original expiry date when you move between paid plans or credit tiers, in either direction. Switching between Pro and Business, or to a different Pro or Business tier, only changes your monthly credit allocation: immediately when you upgrade, and from the next billing period when you downgrade.
>
> For a full overview of credit expiry and rollover, see [Credits and usage](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage.md#general-credits).

> **Can I switch from monthly to annual billing?**
>
> Yes. You can switch from monthly to annual billing on Pro and Business plans from [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing). Select **Yearly** above the plan cards, then select **Switch to annual** on your current plan card.
>
> Annual billing gives you two months free compared with paying monthly. Monthly plan credits on annual billing are still issued monthly and expire 1 month after the annual billing period ends. See [Switch between monthly and annual billing](#switch-between-monthly-and-annual-billing).

> **Can I cancel from the mobile app?**
>
> No. The mobile app has no cancel option. Open **lovable.dev** in your phone's browser and cancel from [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing). Plans purchased inside the iOS app cover one month and do not renew, so you have nothing to cancel. See [Cancel from your phone](#cancel-from-your-phone).

> **Do I get a refund when I cancel?**
>
> No. When you cancel, Lovable stops future charges but does not refund the current billing period. Your plan runs until the period ends. Unused monthly plan credits and rollover credits are frozen rather than refunded, while top-up and bonus credits stay usable until they expire. If you believe you were charged in error, contact the support team. See the [Billing and subscription FAQ](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/support-policy.md#billing-and-subscription) in the Support policy.

## Troubleshooting

> **I upgraded but have not received credits. What should I do?**
>
> You receive additional credits only if you upgrade to a plan or tier with more included credits, and you receive the difference between the two allocations, not a full new grant.
>
> If you upgraded and still have not received the additional credits, contact support at [support@lovable.dev](mailto:support@lovable.dev).

> **I downgraded to Free. Why can't I use some of my credits?**
>
> If you downgrade to the Free plan, unexpired credits granted through your paid subscription are frozen. This means you cannot use monthly plan credits or rollover credits while your workspace is on Free.
>
> If you upgrade to a paid plan again before those credits expire, you can continue using them until their original expiry date.
>
> Top-up credits you purchased while subscribed and any bonus credits can still be used on Free until they expire.
