# Video tutorials and courses

> Watch short video walkthroughs of Lovable features, follow a guided learning path in the Lovable Academy, or browse tutorials from the Lovable community.

Lovable's video tutorials live in the [Lovable Academy](https://academy.lovable.app/academy), a free learning site from the Lovable team. Its [video catalog](https://academy.lovable.app/academy/video-catalog) combines short walkthroughs of individual features, such as the built-in backend, Plan mode, publishing, and custom knowledge, with longer deep dives that build a complete app from the first prompt to a published product.

The Academy also offers a guided learning path. In a series of short lessons, you build and publish a complete app while each concept, from planning to databases to user accounts, arrives at the point where you need it.

> **[Watch the video catalog](https://academy.lovable.app/academy/video-catalog)**
>
> Short feature walkthroughs and long-form deep dives.

> **[Follow the learning path](https://academy.lovable.app/academy/learning-path)**
>
> Short lessons that take you from a blank prompt to a published app.

Watching videos requires no account. Lessons and courses ask you to sign in so that the Academy can save your progress.

## Community tutorials

Lovable users publish their own tutorials on YouTube. The [Lovable Community playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLbVHz4urQBZkJ6WMXlIGc3NGsufrleogY) collects them in one place. Community videos are made by users, so the interface they show can differ from the current product.
