# Welcome to Lovable

> Lovable is a full-stack AI development platform for building, iterating on, and deploying web applications using natural language, with real code, security, and enterprise governance.

![Welcome Lovable Dashboard](https://mintcdn.com/lovable-f9060f1e/ybHXd0Cn2UWhku0A/images/welcome-lovable-dashboard.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=ybHXd0Cn2UWhku0A\&q=85\&s=7063addda22ec0066d45e5ece92559c4)

Lovable lets individuals and teams build production-grade web applications using natural language. You describe what you want to build, and Lovable generates a working application that includes frontend, backend, database, authentication, and integrations, all backed by editable code.

Lovable supports the full lifecycle of modern product development, from early exploration and prototyping to deployment and ongoing operation.

Applications are built inside shared [workspaces](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/workspace), where a group of people builds from one plan and a shared pool of credits, each working on one or more of their own projects. Each project represents a single application and produces a codebase that can be synced to GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket with [Git sync](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/git-sync-overview) and integrated into existing engineering workflows. The platform is built to help people move faster while maintaining ownership, security, and operational control.

You can start for free, and plans are priced by [credits](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage.md), not by projects or seats. See [Subscription plans](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/subscription-plans.md).

## Core concepts

Four terms come up everywhere in Lovable:

* **Account**: Your personal identity on Lovable. You [log in with one account](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/create-an-account.md) and use it across every workspace you belong to.
* **Workspace**: Where you and your team work. A [workspace](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/workspace) holds projects, members, billing, and a shared pool of credits.
* **Project**: One app. Each [project](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/projects/overview) holds its project chat, preview, code, and version history, and lives in exactly one workspace.
* **Chats**: Conversations with Lovable outside your projects, for thinking through ideas, asking about your apps, and working with connected tools. When something needs building, a [chat](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/chats.md) sends the work to a project.

## Why use Lovable

Lovable makes it easier to go from an idea to working software.

**Faster iteration**\
Move from idea to working software by building through natural language instead of starting from a blank codebase.

**Collaboration when you need it**\
Work solo or with a team. Lovable supports shared workspaces so people across roles can contribute in one place when collaboration matters.

**Code ownership and flexibility**\
Keep full ownership of your code and integrate Lovable into existing workflows.

**Built with enterprise needs in mind**\
[Security, privacy, and governance capabilities](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/lovable-for-enterprise.md) are built into the platform from the start.

## Who Lovable is for

Lovable is used by teams inside companies, by technical teams and agencies, across enterprises, and by people building on their own.

**Teams in companies**

* Product managers and designers turning prototypes into internal tools the business runs on
* Operations, sales, marketing, finance, and HR teams replacing spreadsheets and per-seat software with their own apps
* Marketers building landing pages, campaign sites, and dashboards

**Technical teams and agencies**

* Developers using Lovable to quickly set up projects, internal tools, and early prototypes
* Engineering teams reviewing, extending, and maintaining generated code through GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket
* Agencies and consultancies delivering client projects where code ownership and portability matter

**Enterprises**

* Teams building internal tools or business-critical applications used across the organization
* Organizations building and operating applications with [security, compliance, and governance](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/lovable-for-enterprise.md) requirements
* Companies integrating AI-assisted development into established engineering workflows

**Individual builders**

* Founders and entrepreneurs launching products and reaching their first customers
* Independent makers building side projects, prototypes, or small products on their own
* Students and educators working on coursework, learning projects, or experiments

## How Lovable fits into your workflow

Lovable is designed to complement existing tools and processes:

1. Describe what you want to build
2. Review and iterate on your app
3. [Publish](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/publish.md) it to a live URL
4. When you want the code in your own pipeline, sync it to [GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/git-sync-overview) and deploy, operate, and govern to your standards

## Get help

If a project shows an error, ask Lovable in the project chat first. Lovable investigates the problem and can fix it. The full workflow is in [Debug and improve your app](https://docs.lovable.dev/prompting/prompting-debugging.md).

For other questions, the [Support policy](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/support-policy.md) explains every way to get help:

* The docs assistant, which answers from the documentation. Open it from any docs page.
* The [status page](https://status.lovable.dev/), for platform-wide incidents.
* The community on [Discord](https://discord.gg/lovable-dev), open to all plans.
* The support team, through the support form at [lovable.dev/support](https://lovable.dev/support). Direct support is available on paid plans.
