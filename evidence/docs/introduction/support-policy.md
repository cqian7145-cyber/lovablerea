# Support policy

> How to get help with Lovable: self-help resources, what support covers on each plan, how to contact the support team, response times, and enterprise support levels.

This page explains where to get help with Lovable, what the support team covers, and what to expect on each plan.

* Everyone can use the [docs](https://docs.lovable.dev), the docs assistant, the status page, and the community on Discord.
* Paid plans add direct help from the support team.
* Enterprise plans add a dedicated account team.

## What support covers

The support team helps with the Lovable platform and your account, including:

* Billing issues, including refunds and unexpected charges
* Platform bugs
* Account management
* Workarounds or solutions to known issues
* General guidance on using Lovable
* General questions about Lovable

The support team does not cover the following requests. Use the alternative listed with each one instead.

* **Project-specific debugging.** The support team cannot debug the code in your project. Ask Lovable in the project chat, follow the [debugging guide](https://docs.lovable.dev/prompting/prompting-debugging.md), or ask the community on Discord.
* **Implementing third-party or open-source integrations.** If a connector from the catalog is not working as expected, start with its page in the [connector catalog](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/introduction). For a service you integrated yourself, refer to that service's own documentation.
* **Takedowns, abuse reports, and appeals.** If your project was taken down or your account was disabled, the **Trust & Safety** team handles it through the forms at [lovable.dev/abuse](https://lovable.dev/abuse), not the support form. See [If your project was taken down](#if-your-project-was-taken-down).

Other requests belong to another team or company, for example security vulnerabilities, privacy requests, or sales. See [Not covered by support? Where to go](#not-covered-by-support-where-to-go).

## Contact support

To use the support form, you need to be logged in, and your current workspace needs to be on a paid plan. On the Free plan, the **Contact us** button opens an upgrade prompt instead of the form.

**1. Open the support form**

Go to [lovable.dev/support](https://lovable.dev/support) and click **Contact us**. Use the Lovable account that belongs to your paying workspace.

**2. Describe the problem**

Fill in the **Subject** and **Message**. Optionally select a **Project** so its URL is included in the request.

You can attach images, PDF files, and DOC files, up to 5 MB in total.

**3. Read the AI reply**

Lovable's support AI replies within a couple of minutes and resolves most questions.

If the answer does not help, ask for a human. A member of the support team then follows up by email.

If the form is unavailable, or you cannot log in to use it, email [support@lovable.dev](mailto:support@lovable.dev) from the same account email.

### What to include

The support team can start on your request immediately when your first message includes:

* What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
* The project URL, if the issue is tied to one project
* Screenshots or a short recording of the problem
* When it started, and whether anything changed shortly before
* What you have already tried

### Buy or upgrade to Enterprise

To buy an Enterprise plan, or to move an existing workspace to Enterprise, [contact sales](https://lovable.dev/enterprise). The sales team handles pricing and contract questions.

Enterprise plans are arranged with the sales team, not through the support form.

### Contact support from the editor

Enterprise workspaces can open a support request from inside a project, without using the support form. In the [editor](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/projects/editor.md), click the **+** button next to the chat input to open the [Chat actions](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/projects/chat.md#chat-actions) menu, select **Help & support → Contact support**, and describe what is going wrong. This is one of the [Enterprise support](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/lovable-for-enterprise.md#support) features.

You can attach up to 5 JPG or PNG files, with a total size of 5 MB. Lovable attaches your project, workspace, and account details automatically, so the support team has the context without you filling anything in.

The support team replies by email. Everyone with access to the project can use this option.

### Response times on Enterprise plans

Your contract sets out the support terms for your organization, including how quickly Lovable responds. For questions about them, contact your account team. The general conditions are in Sections 10 and 11 of the [Product Terms](https://lovable.dev/product-terms).

## If your project was taken down

Lovable blocks or removes projects that break the [Platform Rules](https://lovable.dev/platform-rules), for example:

* Phishing or impersonation
* Malware
* Illegal goods or services
* Adult content
* Content that infringes someone else's copyright or trademark

The **Trust & Safety** team makes these decisions and reviews appeals, not the support team. Use the appeal forms described below rather than the support form.

**What happens.** You receive an email notification from **Trust & Safety**. Keep it, because the appeal forms refer to the steps in that notification. Depending on the case, Lovable blocks the project or removes it, and the published site is taken down.

**What you can do.** If you believe the decision was a mistake, appeal at [lovable.dev/abuse](https://lovable.dev/abuse), logged in with the account that owns the project. Use the form that matches your notice. **Trust & Safety** does not process appeals filed on the wrong form.

* **Website Takedown Appeal**, for a Platform Rules takedown.
* **Trademark Takedown Appeal**, for a trademark complaint.
* **DMCA Counter-Notification**, for a copyright complaint under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). If the complainant does not notify Lovable within 10 to 14 business days that they have filed a court action, Lovable restores the project. See the [DMCA Copyright Policy](https://lovable.dev/dmca).
* **Disabled Account Appeal**, if your account was disabled.

**Trust & Safety** reviews every appeal and replies by email. Submitting an appeal does not guarantee reinstatement.

To report a project built by someone else, use the report form at the same address.

## FAQ

These are the questions the support team answers most often. Each answer gives the short version and links to the full one.

### Billing and subscription

These entries cover canceling, invoices, refunds, message costs, and what happens when credits run out.

> **How do I cancel my subscription?**
>
> Go to [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing), select **Manage** on your plan card, then **Downgrade to free**. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period. After that, your workspace moves to the Free plan.
>
> On a phone, cancel from **lovable.dev** in your browser. The mobile app has no cancel option.
>
> Plans bought inside the iOS app work differently. Each purchase covers one month and does not renew, so you have nothing to cancel, and your plan card shows **Managed by App Store** instead of **Manage**.
>
> See [Cancel your subscription](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/manage-subscription.md#cancel-your-subscription).

> **Where do I find my invoices and receipts?**
>
> Go to [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing), select **Manage** on your plan card, then **Invoices & payments**. Lovable opens the Stripe billing portal, which lists your invoices, receipts, payment methods, and billing details.
>
> Upcoming invoices and your payment history also appear in **Plans & credit usage**. Payment methods and billing details are stored by Stripe, so the portal is the only place to change them.
>
> Enterprise workspaces billed under a contract do not use the billing portal. Ask your account team for invoices.
>
> See [Invoices and billing](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/invoices-and-billing.md).

> **Can I get a refund?**
>
> Subscription fees and credits are non-refundable, except where the law requires a refund. See the **No Refunds** section of the [Terms of Service](https://lovable.dev/terms).
>
> If you believe you were charged in error, for example a subscription that renewed after you canceled, contact support with your workspace name and the charge in question. The support team reviews billing errors. See [Contact support](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/support-policy.md#contact-support).
>
> When you cancel, Lovable stops future charges but does not refund the current billing period. Your plan runs until the period ends. Unused monthly plan and rollover credits are frozen rather than refunded, while top-up and bonus credits stay usable until they expire. See [What happens when you change or cancel](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/manage-subscription.md#what-happens-when-you-change-or-cancel)

> **I paid, but my plan has not changed. What should I do?**
>
> Check your plan and subscription status in [**Workspace settings → Plans & credit usage**](https://lovable.dev/settings/billing). You can also ask Lovable in the project chat whether your subscription has activated.
>
> If the plan still has not changed, contact support. See [Troubleshooting](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/invoices-and-billing.md#troubleshooting) on the Invoices and billing page.

> **My credits ran out. What stops working?**
>
> You cannot keep building until credits are available. Your published site stays live, and visitors can still open it.
>
> Two features stop working:
>
> * AI features inside deployed apps stop working.
> * Apps that use the built-in backend pause shortly after the balance reaches zero. The built-in backend is the database, login, and file storage Lovable runs for your app. While it is paused, the page still loads, but logins and data stop working.
>
> Add credits, or enable auto top-up on Pro and Business plans. A paused backend does not resume by itself after a top-up. Open **More → Database** in the project and click **Wake up** on the paused card. See [What happens when you run out of credits](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/out-of-credits.md#what-happens-when-you-run-out-of-credits).

> **Why did a message cost more credits than I expected?**
>
> Build mode is priced on the work Lovable does, not on the length of your message. A short request can cost more when Lovable has to explore the codebase, verify the result, check it in the browser, search the web, or generate images.
>
> To see what any message cost, open the **More options** menu below Lovable's response and look under **Credits used**. For long messages, Lovable pauses and checks in with you when a single message passes your check-in level, 20 credits by default.
>
> Plan mode messages cost 1 credit each. See [Build costs](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage.md#build-costs).

> **Does Try to fix cost credits?**
>
> Your account includes 10 free fixes, shared across **Try to fix** on build errors and fixes from the Security view, in all your workspaces and projects. Each free fix becomes available again 24 hours after you use it.
>
> After you have used all 10, **Try to fix** still works, but it is treated as standard build usage and consumes credits. See [Fix errors and get unstuck](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/projects/chat.md#fix-errors-and-get-unstuck).

### Publishing and domains

These entries cover changes that do not appear on the live site, a site that is down, custom domains, and how your site appears in search results and link previews.

> **Why are my changes not on the live site after I published?**
>
> Publishing deploys a snapshot of your project. Lovable does not push later edits live on its own.
>
> To update the live site, click **Publish**, then **Publish changes**. A dot on the **Publish** button shows when you have changes waiting to be deployed.
>
> See [Republish to make new changes live](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/publish.md#republish-to-make-new-changes-live).

> **My published site is down. What should I check?**
>
> Check the [status page](https://status.lovable.dev/) first. If there is no incident, work through these checks:
>
> * Open the **Publish** dialog and confirm the project is still published.
> * If the project is unpublished and your workspace is on the Free plan, Lovable may have unpublished it for inactivity. Check your email for a notice from Lovable, and see [Does my published site expire?](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/publish.md#does-my-published-site-expire) for how to bring the site back.
> * If you use a custom domain, go to **Project → Settings → Domains** and check that its status is **Live**.
> * Ask Lovable in the project chat about the domain. It reports the domain's state.
>
> If everything looks right and the site still does not load, contact support with the project URL and what you see. See [Hosting](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/hosting.md).

> **Why is my custom domain not live, or still showing my old website?**
>
> DNS is the system that points your domain name at a website. DNS changes can take up to 72 hours to reach every network. If you connected the domain recently, you may only need to wait.
>
> If your domain shows an old website, your DNS provider probably still has `A`, `AAAA`, or `CNAME` records from a previous host. These records tell the internet where your domain points. Remove them so that only Lovable's records remain, then check the expected records in **Project → Settings → Domains**.
>
> Ask Lovable in the project chat about the domain. Lovable reports its state, which records it expects, and whether it can see them. See [Troubleshooting domains from other providers](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/custom-domain.md#troubleshooting-domains-from-other-providers).

> **Why does sign-in fail on my custom domain?**
>
> Your app's auth redirect URLs probably do not include the new domain yet. These are the addresses your app is allowed to send users back to after they sign in.
>
> For apps on the built-in backend, Lovable adds the domain automatically when it becomes **Live**. If sign-in still fails, ask Lovable in the project chat to add the domain to the auth redirect URLs. See [Site URL and redirect URLs](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/authentication#site-url-and-redirect-urls).

> **How do I change my site's title, favicon, or social preview image?**
>
> Ask Lovable in the project chat. Lovable generates the site title, the meta description shown under it in search results, the favicon shown in browser tabs, and the social sharing image shown in link previews while it builds your app. Each page can have its own. To change any of them, describe the change in the project chat, for example a new site title or a new social sharing image for the pricing page.
>
> Metadata edits are treated as standard build usage and consume credits. See [Site metadata is generated for you](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/publish.md#site-metadata-is-generated-for-you).

> **My site does not show up on Google. What should I do?**
>
> Google indexing, which adds your pages to its search results, takes from a few hours to a few days after publishing, and sometimes longer. To speed it up, submit your sitemap in Google Search Console, use **URL Inspection** on important pages, and request indexing for new or updated pages.
>
> Run the review in your project's **SEO & AI search** tab to check indexing, metadata, and sitemap issues on the live site. SEO stands for search engine optimization. See [Optimize your app for SEO and AI search](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/seo-aeo.md#faq).

### The built-in backend and your data

These entries cover a paused built-in backend, resuming after a top-up, taking your data with you, and sign-up emails that do not arrive.

> **Why does my app load but login and data stop working?**
>
> The most common cause is a paused built-in backend. The built-in backend is the database, login, and file storage Lovable runs for your app. The page still loads because the frontend is served separately, but the app cannot reach its data.
>
> The built-in backend pauses in these situations:
>
> * Your workspace has no credits left.
> * A credit limit was reached.
> * The project has been inactive for a period of time.
> * You paused it yourself.
> * Lovable paused it because of excessive Cloud usage.
>
> The paused project card in **More → Database** says why the project is paused and what to do next. See [Resume a paused project](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/database-pause-remove.md#resume-a-paused-project).

> **Why is my app still down after I added credits?**
>
> A top-up does not resume a paused backend by itself. Visiting the deployed app or waiting does not resume it either.
>
> Open **More → Database** in the project and click **Wake up** on the paused card.
>
> If the project still does not resume after you wake it up, contact support with the project URL. See [Pause a database you're not using](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/database-pause-remove.md#pause-a-database-youre-not-using).

> **Can I export my data or move off the built-in backend?**
>
> Yes. **Export project data**, under **More → Database → Manage → Settings**, downloads your full database as a single file, an SQL (Structured Query Language) dump, that contains your tables and their data. You can run one export every 24 hours.
>
> The export does not include:
>
> * Files in storage buckets
> * Edge function code, the server-side code Lovable runs for your app
> * Your project's secrets
>
> The export includes your users' accounts and their password hashes, so users keep their passwords if you restore the export into a Supabase project.
>
> You cannot export while the backend is paused, so resume it first. See [Export your database](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/database-backups.md#export-your-database) and [Deploying and hosting outside Lovable](https://docs.lovable.dev/tips-tricks/external-deployment-hosting.md).
>
> Your code is separate from your data. On paid plans, you can take a copy of the code with **Download codebase** in the [code editor](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/code-mode.md). On any plan, you can keep a repository in sync with [Git sync](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/git-sync-overview).

> **Why are my app's sign-up or confirmation emails not arriving?**
>
> Ask your users to check their spam folder first. Email providers often filter messages from new domains there.
>
> If many people sign up at once, your project may have reached its hourly email sending limit. For better deliverability and a branded sender address, set up [custom emails](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/custom-emails.md). See [Troubleshooting](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/email-auth.md#troubleshooting) on the Email authentication page.

### Code and GitHub

These entries cover connecting a repository, bringing in existing code, and downloading your project.

> **How do I connect my project to GitHub?**
>
> A workspace admin or owner first connects the workspace to GitHub. After that, workspace or project admins and owners can link a project from **Project settings → Git → GitHub**, or from **Project → GitHub** in the **Chat actions** menu, the **+** button next to the chat input.
>
> Linking creates a new GitHub repository for the project. The repository is private by default, and each project connects to one repository. See [Link a project to a GitHub repository](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/github.md#link-a-project-to-a-github-repository).

> **Can I import an existing repository or codebase into Lovable?**
>
> No. Git sync exports from Lovable, and connecting a project creates a new repository. Lovable cannot start a project from code that already exists elsewhere. See [Git sync](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/git-sync-overview).

> **How do I download my code?**
>
> On paid plans, anyone with edit access can save the whole project as a `.zip` file. Either click **Download codebase** at the bottom of the code editor's file panel, or use the **Download codebase** section in **Project settings → Git**.
>
> To keep a repository in sync instead, set up [Git sync](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/git-sync-overview) with your Git provider. See [Code editor](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/code-mode.md).

### Account

These entries cover logging in, changing your login email, and deleting your account.

> **What do I do if I cannot log in or forgot my password?**
>
> Log in at [lovable.dev/login](https://lovable.dev/login) with the same method you signed up with: email and password, Google, GitHub, Apple, or SSO. If you are not sure which method you used, enter your email on the login page and Lovable marks the linked method as **Recommended**.
>
> If you signed up with email and password, click **Forgot password?** on the login page to reset it. If you signed up with Google, GitHub, Apple, or SSO, there is no Lovable password to reset. Log in with that provider, and reset your password with the provider if needed. See [Log in to an existing account](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/create-an-account.md#log-in-to-an-existing-account) and [Reset your password](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/create-an-account.md#reset-your-password).
>
> If Lovable shows a **Login denied due to suspicious activity** message, Lovable's automated fraud checks denied the login. Email [support@lovable.dev](mailto:support@lovable.dev) from the account's email address and include the exact message. See [Sign-up or login denied due to suspicious activity](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/create-an-account.md#sign-up-or-login-denied-due-to-suspicious-activity).

> **Can I change the email I log in with?**
>
> Not directly. Create a new account with the email you want, then copy your projects into it by remixing them. A remix brings the code and the database structure, but not database records, custom domains, or the publish state.
>
> See [Move a project to another account](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/projects/remix.md#move-a-project-to-another-account).

> **How do I delete my account?**
>
> Go to [**Account settings → Account → Danger zone**](https://lovable.dev/settings/account) and select **Delete account**.
>
> Lovable first asks you to hand over or delete any workspace where you are the only owner. Deletion then finalizes after a 30-day grace period. During those 30 days, you can log back in and cancel it. After that, your account and its data are permanently removed, and you cannot reactivate the account. See [Delete account](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/delete-account.md).

### Getting support

>

>

> **Can I talk to a human?**
>
> Yes. The first reply to a support request comes from Lovable's support AI, which resolves most questions within a couple of minutes.

> * The [docs](https://docs.lovable.dev), and the docs assistant on every page of docs.lovable.dev
> * The docs assistant in the search box on [lovable.dev/support](https://lovable.dev/support)
> * The community on [Discord](https://discord.gg/lovable-dev)

> **Can support fix a bug in my app?**
>
> No. The support team covers the Lovable platform and your account, not the code in your project.
>
> Ask Lovable in the project chat to investigate, use **Try to fix** on a build error, or [revert to an earlier version](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/projects/history.md#revert-to-an-earlier-version). See [Debug and fix errors](https://docs.lovable.dev/prompting/prompting-debugging.md).
>
> If you would rather have someone build it for you, browse [Lovable experts](https://lovable.dev/experts).

> **My project was taken down. Is that a support request?**
>
> No. Takedowns are handled by **Trust & Safety**, not the support team. Appeals go through the forms at [lovable.dev/abuse](https://lovable.dev/abuse).
>
> See [If your project was taken down](#if-your-project-was-taken-down) for which form to use and what to expect.
